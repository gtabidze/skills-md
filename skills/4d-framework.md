# 4D Framework for AI Collaboration

The 4D Framework helps you thoughtfully structure your AI collaboration by considering four key dimensions: **Delegation**, **Description**, **Discernment**, and **Diligence**. Use this skill to guide yourself through any project where you're working with AI.

## How to Use This Skill

1. **Identify your project type** from the examples below, or describe what you're working on
2. **Work through each 4D category** in order
3. **Write your answers** to the guiding questions
4. **Use your responses** to brief the AI on your expectations and criteria

---

## Research Project

**Scenario:** You're using AI to help analyze a large dataset for a research paper.

### Delegation
*How would you divide the analytical work between yourself and the AI?*

- [ ] Data cleaning and preprocessing
- [ ] Statistical analysis
- [ ] Literature review summaries
- [ ] Hypothesis generation
- [ ] Interpreting results
- [ ] Writing analysis sections
- [ ] Other: ________________

**Your answer:**

---

### Description
*What context would the AI need to understand about your research question to do its share of the tasks well?*

- What is your primary research question?
- What existing literature or frameworks should inform the analysis?
- Are there specific methodologies you must use?
- What data sources and formats are you working with?
- What are your key variables and their definitions?
- What constraints (time, scope, budget) exist?

**Your answer:**

---

### Discernment
*How would you verify the AI's analysis for accuracy?*

- [ ] Compare against manual spot-checks of the data
- [ ] Validate statistical assumptions with domain experts
- [ ] Test conclusions against original data
- [ ] Review methodology for appropriateness
- [ ] Check for logical consistency in interpretation
- [ ] Verify citations and sources
- [ ] Other: ________________

**Your answer:**

---

### Diligence
*What ethical considerations might arise when publishing AI-assisted research?*

- Will you disclose AI use in your methodology?
- How do you maintain intellectual honesty about authorship?
- Are there concerns about bias in AI-assisted analysis?
- What reproduceability issues might emerge?
- How will peer reviewers respond to AI involvement?
- What institutional policies apply?

## Creative Project

**Scenario:** You're collaborating with AI to develop character concepts for a story.

### Delegation
*What creative elements would you want to explore through AI collaboration vs. develop independently?*

- [ ] Character archetypes and backstories
- [ ] Personality traits and motivations
- [ ] Dialogue and voice development
- [ ] Character relationships and dynamics
- [ ] Visual descriptions
- [ ] Character flaws and growth arcs
- [ ] Other: ________________

**Your answer:**

---

### Description
*How might you guide the AI to generate characters that fit your story's world?*

- What is the setting and genre of your story?
- What themes are central to your narrative?
- What existing characters or archetypes should influence new characters?
- Are there cultural, historical, or world-building constraints?
- What role should these characters play in the plot?
- What audience are you writing for?

**Your answer:**

---

### Discernment
*How would you decide which AI-suggested elements to keep, modify, or discard?*

- [ ] Does it align with story themes and tone?
- [ ] Is it original enough for your creative vision?
- [ ] Does it serve the plot effectively?
- [ ] Does it feel authentic to the character?
- [ ] Does it resonate emotionally with you?
- [ ] Does it add complexity or depth?
- [ ] Other: ________________

**Your answer:**

---

### Diligence
*How would you acknowledge AI's contribution to your creative work?*

- Will you disclose AI use in author's notes?
- How much of the final work is AI-generated vs. your own?
- What accountability do you have for the story's content?
- Does AI involvement affect the artistic integrity or copyright?
- Should credits acknowledge the AI tool used?
- What's your personal stance on AI in creative work?

## Tips for Success

1. **Be specific** - Vague briefs lead to misaligned outputs
2. **Document your thinking** - Writing answers forces clarity
3. **Iterate** - Use early AI outputs to refine your 4Ds
4. **Stay honest** - Acknowledge both capabilities and limitations
5. **Review continuously** - Revisit your 4D answers as the project evolves
