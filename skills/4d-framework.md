# 4D Framework Skill

Use this skill when working on a project with AI by mentioning it. The 4D Framework will guide you to think through your collaboration clearly.

## The 4D Dimensions

### 1. **Delegation** 
What parts of this project will you do vs. what will you ask the AI to help with?

### 2. **Description**
What context, vision, and expectations do you need to communicate to the AI so it understands what you want?

### 3. **Discernment**
What criteria will you use to evaluate whether the AI's output actually meets your needs?

### 4. **Diligence**
What ethical, legal, or responsible considerations matter for this type of work?

---

## How to Use This Skill

**Simply tell me:**
- What project you're working on
- What you want to use AI for

**I will:**
- Ask you targeted questions for each 4D dimension
- Help you clarify your thinking
- Ensure you're ready to brief the AI effectively and evaluate its work

## Quick Examples of Projects

- Writing emails or marketing copy
- Analyzing data or research
- Creating content (writing, design, code)
- Planning or organizing work
- Learning new topics
- Brainstorming ideas
- Building something technical

*The 4D Framework works for ANY project where you collaborate with AI.*
