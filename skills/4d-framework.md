# 4D Framework for AI Collaboration

The 4D Framework helps you think clearly about how to work effectively with AI. Use it before delegating tasks to AI—it ensures you've thought through the collaboration from all angles.

---

## The Four Dimensions

### 1. **Delegation**
*Decide: what's human work, what's AI work?*

**Why it matters**: The best AI outcomes happen when humans stay in control of decisions, judgment calls, and quality gates. Understanding where to draw the line prevents over-reliance and keeps you accountable.

**Questions to think about:**
- Which parts require your judgment, domain expertise, or decision-making?
- What can AI do better/faster (research, drafting, synthesis)?
- Where do you need to review or edit the AI's work?
- Are there parts you want to do yourself for learning or quality reasons?

---

### 2. **Description**
*Communicate: what context does the AI need?*

**Why it matters**: AI output quality depends almost entirely on how well you describe what you want. Good description includes your vision, constraints, success criteria, and relevant context.

**Questions to think about:**
- What's the goal or end state you're aiming for?
- What constraints or requirements exist (tone, format, audience, compliance)?
- What does good output look like? (share examples if possible)
- What context does the AI need to know (your role, audience, background)?
- Are there things the AI should *not* do or assume?

---

### 3. **Discernment**
*Evaluate: how will you judge whether the output is good?*

**Why it matters**: You need concrete criteria to assess AI work—not just "does it feel right?" Clear evaluation prevents you from accepting mediocre outputs and helps you give useful feedback.

**Questions to think about:**
- What specific things must be true in the output?
- How will you know if it meets your needs (clarity, accuracy, completeness)?
- What's a dealbreaker vs. something you can fix?
- Will you need to iterate, or does it need to be right the first time?

---

### 4. **Diligence**
*Reflect: what ethical, legal, or responsible considerations apply?*

**Why it matters**: Not every project is appropriate for AI assistance, and not every use is equally responsible. Thinking this through upfront prevents problems later.

**Questions to think about:**
- Are there privacy, security, or confidentiality concerns?
- Could this create compliance or legal risks?
- Are you comfortable with the AI's training data and usage?
- Is there a human stakeholder who should know you used AI?
- Could this work harm someone if done carelessly?

## Quick Tip

The strongest outcomes happen when you're **deliberate** about delegation (don't outsource decisions), **clear** about what you want, **rigorous** about evaluation, and **thoughtful** about responsibilities. This framework helps you do all four.
